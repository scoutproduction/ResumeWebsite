I want to understand how intelligent systems work, not just how to use them.

My interest in AI comes from the intersection of mathematics, computation, and the study of human systems. I enjoy working from the underlying architecture of a model through its implementation and into a practical application.

Mamba from scratch: selective state-space models

A from-scratch PyTorch implementation of Mamba and a Transformer comparison. The research examines selective memory, parameter efficiency, initialization, and controlled sequence modeling tasks.
